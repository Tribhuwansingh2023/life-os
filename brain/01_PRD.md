# 01 // PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
LIFE//OS is a gamified life operating system built to conquer the "dopamine gap" of traditional productivity tools. Whereas conventional to-do apps make personal discipline feel like administrative data entry, LIFE//OS transforms real-world habits, projects, workouts, and intellectual pursuits into an immersive, living RPG world.

## 2. TARGET AUDIENCES
1. **Developers, Builders, & Creatives:** High-performance individuals who thrive on measurable system mastery, tech-tree progression, and dark UI aesthetics.
2. **Gamers with Real-World Ambition:** Users who recognize the addictive feedback loops of RPGs (Diablo, Final Fantasy, Cyberpunk) and want that psychological drive applied to health, career, and mental discipline.
3. **Burnout-Prone Achievers:** Users requiring active life-balance diagnostics to prevent over-indexing on work at the expense of wellness and recovery.

## 3. CORE PRODUCT MODULES
- **Command Center (Dashboard):** High-density situational awareness combining player level, active daily quest stack, momentum gauge, raid boss progress, and world territory status.
- **Quest Matrix & Quest Forge:** Modular mission tracking supporting Daily Rites, Epic Milestones, Habit Loops, and Raid Boss Strikes with rank difficulties (E through S) and multi-attribute rewards.
- **Character & 6 Core Attributes:** Strength, Intellect, Discipline, Creativity, Wellness, and Social. Polynomial exponential leveling curve preventing trivial endgame inflation.
- **World Topology Biosystems:** Interactive territory map (5 biomes: Iron Citadel, Chrono Archive, Cybernetic Nexus, Neon Spire, Aether Haven). Territory influence dynamically rises and decays based on real habit consistency.
- **Oracle (AI Game Master):** Continuous behavioral heuristic engine that diagnoses life imbalances (e.g. high intellect but neglected wellness) and suggests strategic recovery directives.
- **Chrono Replay (7-Day Debrief):** Animated weekly telemetry review visualizing XP velocity histograms, momentum stability, and unlocked milestones.
- **Virtual Armory & Treasury:** In-game gold economy for purchasing character relics, passive productivity buffs, and displaying verified milestone badges.
- **System Config & Sensory Engine:** Procedural Web Audio synthesizer, hotkey mappings, and callsign personalization.
