- Command Center dashboard integrating character metrics, momentum gauge, raid boss progress, and today's quest stack.
- Quest Matrix supporting Daily, Epic, Habit, and Boss Raid objectives with difficulty ranks (E through S).

- Chrono Replay 7-day velocity chart with daily XP histogram.
- Armory & Treasury with functional gold purchasing and relic equipment toggles.
- Tactical hotkey matrix (`1-8`, `Q`, `M`, `?`).
- Full `/brain/` architecture documentation suite (18 spec documents).
