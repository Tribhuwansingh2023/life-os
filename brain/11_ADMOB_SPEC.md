# 11 // ADMOB & MONETIZATION SPECIFICATION

## STATUS
**NOT IN SCOPE FOR HACKATHON MVP**

## RATIONALE
LIFE//OS is designed as a focused, premium, distraction-free productivity environment. Intrusive display advertisements or third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) degrade the immersive RPG atmosphere, compromise user privacy, and violate the core ethos of self-mastery.

Future monetization will center on ethical SaaS tiers (e.g. multi-device cloud sync, team guild raids, advanced AI coaching modules) rather than ad networks.
